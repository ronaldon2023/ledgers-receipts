# Y Combinator pitch notes

YC's advice is written for investor Demo Days and the posts date from 2016. The advice on being clear still holds. Sources: [A Guide to Demo Day Presentations](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/guide-to-demo-day-pitches/) and [How to Pitch Your Company](https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/how-to-pitch-your-company/).

## What they say

- Open with what you do, in words the audience already understands. Example: "We deliver groceries to customers in their homes."
- Right after, say what you are building and for whom.
- Include a simple description of the problem, but don't dwell on it. Otherwise listeners wonder what you do until half the pitch is over.
- Explain what the product does quickly and clearly.
- Listeners remember at most 3 or 4 points. Say them explicitly at the end.
- Keep slides to a few words. More than 7 words on a slide is likely too many.
- Make it "baby food": no jargon, no marketing words. "You don't need to sound cool. You need to be clear."
- Email test: write two sentences, send them to a smart friend, and ask them to explain it back.
- Live demos and recorded videos are discouraged at Demo Day. For a hackathon we still show one, so make it short and make it unable to fail (replay mode).

## How we apply it

- Scene 1 of the storyboard is who, problem, fix, in that order.
- The close repeats three points: logged, blocked, checked by code.
- The slides hold three short lines each.
